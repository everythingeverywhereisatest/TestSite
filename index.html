/ 08 / FAQ

Asked up front.
Answered plainly.

What do I need to send you?

Your raw footage, any logos or music you want used, and a few examples of videos you like. A short note on where the video will be posted helps too.

How long does an edit take?

It depends on the length and the amount of footage. We agree on a delivery date in the brief, before work starts.

Can you work with phone footage?

Yes. Most short-form content is shot on phones, and color and sound work make a big difference.

Do you do regular monthly content?

Yes. Ongoing edits for creators and brands are a big part of the work. Tell us how many videos a month you need.

What formats do you deliver?

Whatever the platform needs: 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, 16:9 for YouTube, and square or 4:5 for feeds.
